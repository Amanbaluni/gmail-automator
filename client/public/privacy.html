MailPilot Privacy Policy

MailPilot is a personal Gmail automation tool operated by AmanBaluni.

What it accesses: With your permission, MailPilot reads and modifies your Gmail messages and labels, and sends email on your behalf, in order to run the rules you create.

What it stores: Your rules, templates, activity logs, and an encrypted Google access token, in a private database. Email content is processed only to apply your rules. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Sharing: Data is not shared with third parties, except the hosting and database providers that run the app (Vercel and MongoDB Atlas).

Deleting your data: You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and ask for stored data to be deleted by contacting [YOUR EMAIL].
